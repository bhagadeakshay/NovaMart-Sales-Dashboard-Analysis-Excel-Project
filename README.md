# Sales Dashboard Analysis – Excel Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel for a company that sells computer hardware and accessories across multiple regions, sales channels, and customer types.

The goal was to give management a clear, visual overview of sales performance so they can quickly review:
- Total revenue, orders, quantity sold, and average order value
- Monthly revenue trends
- Regional performance
- Product category contribution
- Sales channel mix
- Top-selling products

## 🔍 Key Insights from the Dashboard

- **East** region generated the highest revenue ($38.76M), followed by West ($31.49M).
- **Computers** is by far the strongest product category ($98.20M), contributing ~65% of total revenue.
- **Corporate Sales** and **Marketplace** are the top two sales channels.
- **Gaming Laptop** is the highest revenue-generating product ($28.62M).
- Revenue peaked in **April** ($15.36M) and dipped in **July** ($9.25M).

## 🛠️ Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards**: Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Quantity, Average Revenue
- **Line Chart**: Monthly Revenue Trend
- **Bar Charts**: Revenue by Region, Revenue by Product Category, Top Products
- **Donut Chart**: Revenue by Sales Channel
- **Interactive Slicers**:
  - Region (North, South, West, East, Central)
  - Product Category
  - Sales Channel
  - Customer Type (New / Returning)
  - Date filters (Month, Year, Quarter, Day)

## 📈 How to Use

1. Open the Excel file (`Indian_Sales_Data.xlsx`).
2. Go to the **dashboard** sheet.
3. Use the slicers on the left to filter by Region, Product Category, Sales Channel, or Customer Type.
4. All charts and KPI cards will update automatically.
